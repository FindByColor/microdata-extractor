Product Description

The unique material of the UA Clone upper adapts to your foot's shape and movements for a more precise fit. It also helps keep your foot centered, making sure you get the most out of the soft, responsive UA HOVR™ cushioning.

  • Responsive UA HOVR™ cushioning reduces impact, returns energy, and helps propel you forward
  • Breathable upper with strategic synthetic overlays for added support & durability where you need it
  • External heel strap for a locked-in fit

    I Love These--Super Comfortable

    I can't tell you how comfortable these shoes are--or actually, I CAN tell you. I've worn earlier HOVR versions, they are my go-to shoes, and I've now worn these the past three days so I could judge. These shoes cradle your feet snugly, but not too snugly, more like a swaddle, and the bottom cushioning is ample, springy but yet firm enough. I wear 9s in UA, 8-1/2s for some other brands. I got 9s for these, based on other reviewers, and while they might be just a smidge narrow in the toebox, it loosened up by the second try. The laces feel good, tie well and are not too long once tied. The graphite/red/orange looks stylish. For those of you, like me, who have liked earlier versions of HOVR, these will wrap around your feet a bit more, in a comfortable way, and the soles will feel terrific. I've tried shoes of several other popular brands other people swear by, and they do NOT feel good on my feet. These do. And that's what counts, over the long run. I'm sold.
