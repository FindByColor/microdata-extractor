My Favorite Trail Runners Ever?

It’s been a while since I’ve had trail runners I’ve loved this much. I ran on Adidas/Salomon’s for years, but with the discontinuation of the Duramo TR and the Salomon Speedcross now feeling too narrow, I tried Hoka. Three pairs, and was only able to eek out 500 miles out of all of them since they’d fall apart – the lower adhesive didn’t seem to hold up. I switched to Altra’s for the width. I’ve got about 1,000 miles on Altra’s and I liked the Altra Superior 4’s but the toe slap is distracting – like I’m wearing flippers. Needless to say, I’ve felt like it’s been a while since I’ve had trail runners I love. So far I’ve got about 100 miles on the SWT’s and they still look like new. The tongue-less upper might be my favorite feature. It’s just snug enough that I can effectively run without any laces on everything from extended or steep downhills. Tightening up the laces makes them perfect. I’m hoping I can get 400+ miles out of these but, needless to say, the TrailRunner SWT’s are my new go-to. Highly recommend.

October 8, 2021
